# PSHR Architecture v0.24 (Public Release Candidate)

Released November 17, 2026. Built on v0.23 of November 5, 2026.

## What is new

- The reproductive health boundary now covers procedures. Folder 07-procedures gains a subfolder reproductive/ for reproductive health procedures, held behind the same boundary as reproductive health laboratory results, conditions, medications, and visits.
- A permission that names reproductive health opens all five places; no broad grant opens any of them.
- Section 6 (Works With Any AI) adds the rule for a person asking a model about their own record. Entries behind a sharing boundary are left out of what the harness hands the model unless the person turns them on for that conversation. A conforming implementation MUST then show a warning that says where the question will be processed, MUST NOT hand any protected entry to the model until the person clears the warning, and MUST write the action to the access history. The choice lasts for that conversation only, and a saved conversation that draws on a protected entry is kept behind the same boundary as that entry.
- Section 2 (How the Record Is Organized), Section 6, Appendix B (The Record at a Glance), and Appendix C (Where Things Go, A Filing Index) state the rules.
