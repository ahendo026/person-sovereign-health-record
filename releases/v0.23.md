# PSHR Architecture v0.23 (Public Release Candidate)

Released November 5, 2026. Built on v0.22 of October 31, 2026.

## What is new

- One reproductive health boundary across the record. Until v0.22 the boundary covered reproductive health laboratory results only (06-results/labs-reproductive). Exports also bring reproductive health conditions, medications, and visits, which filed into the general folders where a broad grant could read them.
- Folders 03-conditions, 04-medications, and 08-encounters each gain a subfolder reproductive/ for reproductive health conditions, medications, and visits: pregnancy, contraception, fertility, prenatal and postpartum care, and sexually transmitted infections.
- A permission that names reproductive health opens all four places; no broad grant opens any of them. A conforming implementation MUST file these entries there by a deterministic rule over the entry's codes, and MUST NOT let an entry outside the boundary name an entry inside it by path.
- Section 2 (How the Record Is Organized), Appendix B (The Record at a Glance), and Appendix C (Where Things Go, A Filing Index) state the rule.
